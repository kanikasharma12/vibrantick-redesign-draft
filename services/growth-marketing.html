Service overview

The listed marketing activities span organic discovery, paid campaigns and direct audience communication. They include search optimization, PPC, social content, email and influencer work, with content formats such as articles, infographics and video.

Measurement and refinement are also part of the stated scope: the page describes analytics and reporting, conversion analysis and A/B testing, as well as monitoring reviews and managing local business listings.

Capabilities

Search engine optimization

Research keywords and apply on-page and off-page SEO practices to website content and search visibility.

Paid advertising

Create and manage PPC campaigns on Google Ads and social media channels, using audience targeting and campaign optimization.

Social media marketing

Develop social strategies and create or manage channel content, with paid social campaigns where suitable.

Content and email marketing

Plan and distribute blogs, articles, infographics and videos. Create targeted email campaigns using personalization and audience segmentation.

Influencer marketing

Identify and work with influencers to present products or services to their audiences.

Conversion rate optimization

Review user behavior and conversion steps, identify bottlenecks, and use A/B testing and other optimization activities.

Analytics and reporting

Track campaign performance and provide reports with observations and recommendations.

Online reputation management

Monitor online mentions and reviews, respond to customer feedback and manage a brand's online reputation.

Local SEO

Optimize business listings for local searches and manage Google Business Profile visibility for geographic areas.
