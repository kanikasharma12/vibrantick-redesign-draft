E-commerce capabilities

Build and manage online store experiences.

The official service page covers store development and the connected functions involved in operating an online shop.

Service overview

E-commerce work includes both the storefront and the processes supporting transactions and orders. Vibrantick describes building custom stores or connecting stores to established commerce platforms, then integrating checkout and inventory functions.

The source also covers activities after launch, including product and site SEO, analytics, updates, security patches and performance optimization. These areas help keep store content, operations and technical maintenance within the same service scope.

Capabilities

Custom e-commerce development

Design and develop an online store around a business's brand identity and requirements, with scalable, secure store functionality.

Platform integrations

Connect stores with platforms including WooCommerce, Shopify and Magento.

Payment gateway integration

Integrate payment gateways to support checkout options, with encryption and relevant compliance standards for sensitive financial information.

Inventory and order workflows

Track stock levels and product variants, support order fulfillment, and use real-time inventory updates and automated reordering capabilities.

Responsive mobile commerce

Design store pages to adapt across screen sizes, including smartphones and tablets.

E-commerce SEO

Work on product pages, metadata and site structure to support search visibility and qualified organic traffic.

Analytics and reporting

Track store metrics and review customer behavior, sales performance and marketing activity to inform store decisions.

Maintenance and support

Handle website updates, security patches and performance optimization as part of ongoing store support.
