Cloud service capabilities

Plan, operate and optimize cloud environments.

The official service description covers the cloud lifecycle, from platform selection and migration through ongoing management and support.

Service overview

Cloud planning begins with the organization's goals and technical requirements. Vibrantick describes choosing among public, private and hybrid environments with performance, security and compliance needs in mind.

The service also covers moving applications, data and workloads and then managing the resulting infrastructure. Operations may include monitoring, capacity adjustment, cost review, governance and recovery planning, depending on the environment.

Capabilities

Strategy and consulting

Align cloud strategy with business goals and choose a public, private or hybrid platform based on performance, security and compliance requirements.

Migration

Move applications, data and workloads from on-premises or existing cloud environments, with attention to data integrity and minimizing downtime.

Infrastructure management

Monitor and manage cloud infrastructure, using auto-scaling and load balancing to respond to changing demand and support availability.

Cost optimization and governance

Review resource utilization and consider right-sizing and reserved instances. Establish policies for access, permissions and cloud resource use.

Security and compliance

Apply security measures, assess cloud environments and address applicable industry standards and regulatory requirements.

Backup and disaster recovery

Plan backups and recovery processes for critical data, and test disaster recovery plans.

Automation and performance

Automate routine tasks, monitor applications and resources, identify bottlenecks, and review configurations and resource allocation.

Vendor management and support

Coordinate with cloud providers and help manage cloud-related support requests and escalations.
